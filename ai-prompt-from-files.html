Why AI answers miss the point

Most off-target answers come from missing context. What the AI needs is usually spread across screenshots, PDFs, notes and links, and pasting them in piece by piece tends to create three problems:

  • Gaps: the AI never sees the document you are looking at.
  • Conflicts: two versions of a file give two different numbers.
  • Leaks: names, numbers or addresses slip into the chat without you noticing.

Step by step: turn files into an AI-ready brief

  1. Add screenshots, PDFs, files, copied text, notes or links. Mix and match as many sources as you need.
  2. AI Brief runs OCR, PDF reading, duplicate checks, fact extraction and privacy detection locally with Apple frameworks.
  3. Review the facts. Every fact keeps its source and confidence, and AI Brief points out conflicts and open questions so you can correct anything that needs context.
  4. Decide on each detected personal detail: keep, replace or remove it. Nothing is removed automatically, and replacements are applied at export.
  5. Choose what stays in the final brief, copy it in one tap and paste it into any AI chat.

Prompt templates to paste after your brief

Once the brief is in the chat, add one of these lines:

  • "Using only the information in this brief, summarize the key points as a list and cite the source for each."
  • "Before answering, point out any facts in this brief that conflict with each other."
  • "Answer the open questions in this brief. If the information is not enough, say what is missing."

Purpose-built templates in the app help you start each brief in the same structure, so your prompts stay consistent from one project to the next.
