Why one prompt beats many pastes

Most off-target AI answers come from missing context. The useful details are spread across screenshots, PDFs, notes and links; pasting them piece by piece creates duplicates, contradictions and can leak personal details.

  • Incomplete context: the AI cannot see the document in front of you.
  • Conflicting facts: two versions of a file give two different numbers.
  • Exposed personal data: a name, number or address slips into the pasted text.

How to make a prompt from files with AI Brief

  1. Add screenshots, PDFs, files, copied text, notes or links; you choose what stays in the final brief.
  2. Text recognition (OCR), PDF reading, duplicate checks, fact extraction and personal-data detection run on your device using Apple frameworks.
  3. Check each fact: it keeps its source and confidence; correct anything that needs context, and look for contradictions and open questions.
  4. For each detected personal detail, choose keep, replace or remove. Nothing is removed automatically; replacements and removals apply on export.
  5. Copy the finished brief in one tap and paste it into the AI chat you already use.

FAQ

Is AI Brief an AI chatbot?

No. AI Brief prepares the context as one brief on your device; you copy it and paste it into any AI chat.

Which files can I turn into a prompt?

Screenshots, PDFs, files, copied text, notes and links, combined however you like.

Is personal data removed automatically?

No. The app detects it and you choose to keep, replace or remove each item; changes apply on export. AI Brief does not promise anonymity.
