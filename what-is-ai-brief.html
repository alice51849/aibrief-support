What is AI Brief?

AI Brief turns screenshots, PDFs, files, copied text, notes and links into one well-organized AI prompt, with the goal, facts and sources sorted. Every fact keeps its source and confidence, so you can check it before an AI sees it. You then copy the finished brief in one tap and paste it into any AI chat.

Text recognition (OCR), PDF reading, duplicate checks, fact extraction and personal-data detection run on your device using Apple frameworks. AI Brief is not an AI chatbot: it prepares the context, and you paste the result into the AI service you already use.

Who is it for?

It suits anyone who regularly hands documents to an AI assistant and wants better answers with less copy-and-paste: people summarizing PDFs, describing a technical problem with screenshots, writing a support request, analyzing a document, preparing study material or following up after a meeting. The built-in templates cover most of these cases.

How much does it cost?

You can start without a purchase: each brief can use up to 5 sources, and you can copy, share and save one brief. A one-time in-app purchase lets you copy, share and save every brief, raises the limit to 50 sources per brief, and adds more templates, detailed long-context briefs, advanced privacy replacements and complete version history. Check the current price on the App Store page, since it can vary by country.
